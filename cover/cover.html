The myth

The cloud migration worked. Teams ship faster, the auditors are satisfied, and nobody made a bad decision. The bill simply grows — with every new service, every successful launch, every environment someone created on a Friday and never deleted.

The myth is not that the cloud is expensive. It is narrower, and more costly to believe: that renting stays cheaper as you grow. Own Your Cloud is the story of one CTO finding the moment that stopped being true — and of a working sovereign-Kubernetes platform built from open-source parts, where every number in the book was measured, not quoted.

“At scale, for steady workloads, the rent can cost two to three times the cost of owning.”
